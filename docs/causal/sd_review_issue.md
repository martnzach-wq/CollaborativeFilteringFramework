# Draft issue for martnzach-wq/KNOWING-integrated-SD-model (decision D16)

**Title:** Review items from the regsim SD bridge: calibration, value of time, population inputs, fuel-cost input

The regsim SD bridge (`regsim causal sd`, perturbation protocol in `regsim.causal.sd_knowing`) ran the model for TALLINN, GRANOLLERS and NAPLES at commit f9e5169: each driver scaled by +10 %, elasticity at 2050; Tallinn also with `apply_cdd_baseattr_compensation = False`; population scaled internally only and as one group of all mobility population inputs. Five items for the modellers:

1. **GC-neutral calibration absorbs time-constant inputs (Tallinn).** With the calibration on, cooling degree days and parking cost have no effect, and fuel cost lowers active-mode km (−0.34). With it off: CDD → active km −0.13, parking → car-km −0.08, fuel cost → car-km −0.11 and active km +0.09. Elasticities of level inputs from Tallinn cannot be transferred or compared with data. Options: calibrate with inputs at a reference level and express them as deviations, or document that only changes after 2019 act.
2. **Value of time in the Tallinn branch.** `_generalised_cost` uses a constant `value_of_time_base = 15` and ignores the configured value (mobility.py, Tallinn branch); the response to the value of time is zero in Tallinn (Granollers +0.51 on car-km).
3. **Population inputs are not linked.** Per-capita km divide by the internal population, and energy demand multiplies per-capita km by the population row of the energy workbook. Scaling all mobility population inputs together leaves per-capita outputs unchanged (|e| < 0.03), but total emissions then do not respond to population at all (Tallinn 0.00, Granollers −0.01), because the energy population is a separate input. One population input, or a link between the two, would make population scenarios consistent.
4. **Fuel-cost input definitions differ.** Tallinn uses the series `policy_car_costs_fuel`, Granollers and Naples the constant `car_cost_per_km_fuel_constant` (which overrides the series). A common input makes scenario and perturbation runs comparable. With the constant perturbed, Granollers fuel cost → car-km is −0.28, close to the long-run literature value (−0.3).
5. **Naples input file.** Resolved: `EN_MEADCityInput_Naples.xlsx` is now in the repository and NAPLES runs (lever order on car-km 2050: road pricing −0.05 > cycling −0.01 > parking −0.01; area toll −0.28).

Lever effects (PI = 1, lever held at its 2019 value, car-km per capita 2050): road pricing −0.42 (Tallinn), −0.72 (Granollers), −0.05 (Naples); cycling attractiveness −0.05, −0.21, −0.01; parking adaptation −0.03, −0.01, −0.01. The ranking agrees across the cities (Spearman 0.94 for Tallinn and Granollers); the sizes differ by up to a factor of four.
